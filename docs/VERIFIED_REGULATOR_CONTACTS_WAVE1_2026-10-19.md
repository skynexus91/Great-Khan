## Confirmed distinctions
- Nigeria SEC's **2026 new-entrant exception** matters: do not direct a new KHAN applicant exclusively to the ePortal without confirmation.
- Brazil's BCB **IN 704/2026** addresses documentation and procedures for SPSVA authorization; review updated consolidated version with Brazilian counsel.
- FIU-IND **reporting-entity registration** is not blanket approval to issue or market a cryptocurrency.
- Romania's ASF general mailbox and petition channel are for enquiries; competence under MiCA and any relevant Romanian implementing legislation must be established for the precise activity.
