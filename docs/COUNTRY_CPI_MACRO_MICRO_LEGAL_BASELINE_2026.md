## Regulatory findings verified from primary authorities (2026)
- **Brazil:** Banco Central do Brasil reports virtual-asset service provider rules, including Resolution BCB 520 effective 2026-02-02, and authorization under BCB 519. Activity classification and applicable authorization still require local counsel. https://www.bcb.gov.br/en/pressdetail/2639/nota
- **Nigeria:** Presidential Executive Order on Virtual Assets Coordination dated 2026-07-18 announces coordination and planned CBN regulatory sandbox; future sandbox participation is NOT authorization today. https://statehouse.gov.ng/president-tinubu-signs-executive-order-on-virtual-assets-establishes-council-to-harmonise-regulation-of-digital-economy/
- **South Africa** (regional regulatory comparator, not in inflation candidate list): SARB/National Treasury published a **draft**, not final, cross-border crypto-assets manual on 2026-08-03. https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/media-releases/2026/crypto-assets

**All other countries:** jurisdiction-specific current legal position **NOT VERIFIED** in this research pass. Do not infer permission from silence. Review issuance, CASP/VASP, custody, transfers, payments/FX, AML/CFT, taxes, data privacy, consumer protection, sanctions and advertising against official dated sources.

## Known limitations
This is a **verified first pass** with 16 comparable CPI observations, 7 missing/stale countries and 3 confirmed regulatory developments. It is NOT a complete 2026 macro/micro dataset, a verified subnational economic report, or 23 country-specific legal opinions. The complete country-by-country exercise requires further primary-source research.
