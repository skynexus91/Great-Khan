## Application dossier checklist
- Legal entity and beneficial owners; jurisdictions of establishment and activity.
- Exact KHAN token rights, white paper and technical architecture.
- Business model: issuance, offer, marketing, custody, transfers, exchange and treasury distributions separately.
- Financial projections, governance and responsible management.
- AML/CFT and sanctions program, risk assessment and transaction controls.
- Customer protection, complaints, fraud and safeguarding procedures.
- Cybersecurity, smart-contract audits, disaster recovery and incident response.
- Privacy notices, data protection impact assessment if needed.
- Tax/accounting policies and external legal classification memo.
- Country-specific official forms, translations, notarizations, fees and filings.

## Contact-verification procedure
Visit regulator's **official** site, navigate Contact/Contact Us and Licensing/Registration, record page URL and last-checked date, cross-check application channel and physical address, and obtain written counsel confirmation. Never send identity documents, company funds or application fees to unverified addresses or personal emails.
