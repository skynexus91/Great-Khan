# Global crypto-asset regulatory expansion — India, Latin America and Africa
Updated 2026-10-19. **Research inventory, not legal advice or verification of current law.** Country status is intentionally marked **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** throughout; no market is deemed authorized. Confirm law, regulator notices, dates and business-specific obligations with locally licensed counsel before advertising, offering, listing, transferring real assets, custody or exchange services.

## Global scope and method
Covers India; **all 20 sovereign Latin American countries** using the common Spanish/Portuguese/French-language geographic definition (including Haiti), plus Belize, Guyana and Suriname as a wider Latin America/Caribbean business expansion scope; and **all 54 internationally recognized African sovereign states**, including North Africa and sub-Saharan Africa. The Caribbean island states not ordinarily classified as Latin America are out of the main list and require a separate Caribbean review. Definitions of Latin America vary; this is an explicitly inclusive planning perimeter rather than a claim about a single canonical list.

For **every** country, counsel must determine: (1) token classification and permissible issuance, (2) public offering/white paper, (3) virtual asset service provider or exchange/custody licensing, (4) AML/CFT and sanctions/travel rule, (5) payment and foreign-exchange restrictions, (6) advertising and consumer protection, (7) tax and reporting, (8) data privacy, (9) banking access, and (10) cross-border marketing and enforcement. Distinguish non-custodial testnet software from real-money services; permission in one country does not passport into another.

## India — priority standalone jurisdiction
**LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED:** Evaluate applicable virtual digital asset (VDA) tax provisions, TDS withholding on relevant transfers, FIU-IND AML/PMLA obligations and reporting for covered activities, advertising/consumer-protection standards, RBI positions on payment use, FEMA cross-border restrictions, and privacy obligations including the DPDP framework and implementing status. Legal characterization depends on KHAN rights, service functions and issuer entity. Do not assert that token issuance, exchange or payments are automatically permitted or prohibited. Suggested official sources to verify: Ministry of Finance, CBDT/Income Tax Department, FIU-IND, RBI, MeitY, Gazette of India.

### African regulator and cross-border priorities
- Nigeria: SEC, CBN, NFIU, tax and local advertising; assess evolving VASP rules.
- South Africa: FSCA, FIC, SARB and tax; assess crypto-asset financial-service requirements.
- Kenya: CBK, CMA, FRC, KRA and applicable new legislation/commencement.
- Ghana: Bank of Ghana, SEC, FIC and local developments.
- Mauritius: FSC and FIU; VASP/initial token offering framework applicability.
- Morocco, Algeria, Egypt and Tunisia: especially verify local prohibitions/restrictions, FX and banking notices before any distribution.
- WAEMU/UEMOA (BCEAO) and CEMAC (BEAC/COBAC): regional monetary and banking regimes may add requirements; national rules still need separate review.
- African Continental Free Trade Area does **not** automatically authorize crypto-asset services continent-wide.
