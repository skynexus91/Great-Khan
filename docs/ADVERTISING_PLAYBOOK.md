# Advertising playbook — The Great Khan
Status: campaign concepts ONLY. No paid advertising has been purchased or launched.

## Mandatory ad disclosure
Always label the product as an experimental simulation. No real currency, earnings, trading, custody, blockchain launch, or investment opportunity. Legal review is required before marketing any real-value financial functionality.

## Creative concepts
**A — Transparency / developer audience**
Headline: “Every simulated KHAN has a trail.”
Body: “Explore open-source transfers, exact accounting and repeatable tests. The Great Khan is a research prototype — not a live cryptocurrency.”
Visual: wallet → transfer → ledger → treasury flow.
CTA: “View GitHub”.

**B — Community / governance audience**
Headline: “What if the rules were visible?”
Body: “Explore a proposed three-pillar model: 40% Commons, 30% People, 30% Businesses — applied to net distributable protocol revenue in a simulation.”
Visual: clear three-part allocation graphic.
CTA: “Read the model”.

**C — Mobile / product audience**
Headline: “Explore an economy before it exists.”
Body: “Follow the development of a mobile-first community economy simulator. Open code, documented decisions, no real-money transactions.”
Visual: Android-friendly mockup clearly marked CONCEPT.
CTA: “Follow development”.

## Channel fit and hypotheses
GitHub and developer communities: technical credibility, open-source contributions; organic first. Educational video and social: short demos and transparency explainers; only after working UI. Community newsletters: governance discussions. Search ads: wait for functional landing page and legal review. Avoid speculative finance influencers and return-focused targeting.

## A/B experiment plan (future)
Test transparency-led vs community-led headline with the same landing page and audience; measure qualified documentation visits and engaged demo sessions, not speculative signups. Set budget cap only after explicit owner approval. Pause creatives that create confusion about whether KHAN is live.

## Design specifications
Square 1080×1080 and landscape 1920×1080 variants; high contrast; readable mobile type; prominent “SIMULATION / CONCEPT” label; text-free reusable background assets where possible; do not use fabricated customer testimonials, token price charts or exchange logos.

## Approval checklist
Product truth verified; source links available; privacy notice; financial-claims review; accessibility contrast; platform policy compliance; budget approval; tracking consent; community feedback process.
