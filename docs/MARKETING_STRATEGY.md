# Marketing strategy — The Great Khan
2026-10-19. Pre-launch, simulation only.

## Positioning
**The Great Khan: a transparent, community-shaped economic simulator.**
Differentiators to validate: understandable KHAN accounting, visible fee routing, three-pillar net-revenue model, and human-reviewed governance. No unsupported claims of production decentralization, real-world token value or returns.

## Audiences and jobs to be done
Community users: understand where contributions go, test governance, and learn economic rules without risking money. Small businesses: explore hypothetical token/community programs. Developers: inspect and test open-source economic invariants. Researchers and community organizers: examine decision accountability and distribution fairness.

## Message architecture
Promise: *Explore how a community economy could work before putting real value at risk.*
Evidence: published source, documented formulas, reproducible randomized tests, CI history.
Tone: ambitious, transparent, inclusive, technically grounded.
Avoid: “invest now”, “guaranteed profit”, “secure blockchain”, “real wallet”, “fully decentralized” or “audited” until those statements are demonstrably true.

## Acquisition and community plan
Phase 1 (now): GitHub README, architecture diagrams, demo walkthroughs, changelog, issues labeled good-first-issue. Phase 2 (prototype): mobile demo and onboarding with clear simulation disclosures; recruit 10–20 usability participants. Phase 3 (validated pilot): community forums, developer tutorials and partner workshops. Any paid acquisition waits for a usable public demo and compliance review.

## Content pillars
1. Build in public: technical progress and tests.
2. Fairness by design: transparent 40/30/30 distribution of net distributable revenue.
3. Responsible governance: human decisions, rights and appeals.
4. Mobile accessibility: inclusive interaction and low-bandwidth design.
5. Honest limits: simulation, risks, and independent validation.

## Measurement
Track opt-in demo activations, onboarding completion, task success, issue contributions, documentation engagement and 7-day return visits. Do not equate social impressions with product-market fit. Publish a monthly evidence-backed progress report.

## Sample copy
Headline: “The Great Khan — Build a fairer economy in simulation.”
Subhead: “Explore transparent KHAN transfers, community rules and a three-pillar allocation model. Open source. Experimental. No real money.”
CTA: “Explore the code” / “Follow development”.
