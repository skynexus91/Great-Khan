# KHAN legal and compliance roadmap — Romania / European Union
Date: 2026-10-19. **Research and issue-spotting only; not legal advice.** Laws and guidance must be verified against current primary sources by qualified counsel before launch.

## Present status
KHAN is a prototype simulator with no verified live token, sale, custody or exchange. No claim is made that it is authorized, licensed, regulated, audited or suitable for investment.

## Legal classification decision tree
1. Is KHAN a crypto-asset within EU MiCA, or an excluded asset? Examine rights, issuer, transferability, redemption, marketing and functionality.
2. Could KHAN qualify as an asset-referenced token (ART), e-money token (EMT), or financial instrument under another regime? Avoid assuming a generic ERC-20 token is automatically a simple utility token.
3. Is there an offer to the public or admission to trading? Assess white-paper, notification, marketing and liability requirements, plus available exemptions, if applicable.
4. Will the team provide custody, exchange, transfer services, execution, advice or another crypto-asset service? Evaluate CASP authorization, outsourcing and safeguarding duties.
5. Are anti-money-laundering, counter-terrorist financing, sanctions, travel-rule or transaction-monitoring obligations triggered by the proposed business model?
6. Are personal data processed? GDPR lawful basis, minimization, retention, rights, DPIA and incident response may apply.
7. Assess consumer protection, unfair commercial practices, advertising disclosures, tax/accounting and payment-service rules.
8. Determine relevant Romanian authorities, corporate structure and cross-border distribution restrictions with counsel.

## Launch restrictions pending review
- No token presale, public fundraising, trading, exchange listings or real-value custody through this prototype.
- No statements promising yield, price growth, asset backing, guaranteed redemption or regulatory approval.
- No unverified claim that treasury distribution is legally equivalent to dividends, charitable donations or investment income.
- No collection of sensitive identification documents before privacy/security design and lawful basis are established.

## Required deliverables
- Written legal classification memo based on actual token rights and code.
- Jurisdiction and entity matrix (Romania / other EU member states / outside EU).
- Draft MiCA white-paper applicability analysis and marketing checklist, if relevant.
- CASP services mapping and authorization assessment.
- AML/CFT and sanctions controls scoped to actual activities.
- GDPR data inventory, privacy notice, retention schedule and vendor agreements.
- Tax/accounting policy for fees, treasury and distributions.
- Consumer complaints and dispute resolution workflow.
- Counsel-approved launch checklist and sign-offs.

## Responsible release gates
**G0:** testnet-only prototype; clear no-value disclosures.
**G1:** technical design and governance draft reviewed internally.
**G2:** external counsel determines applicable regulatory classification and required filings/permissions.
**G3:** independent security assessment and operational controls.
**G4:** only then consider public real-value issuance, promotions or services.

## Primary sources to verify with counsel
- EU Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 on markets in crypto-assets (MiCA), EUR-Lex.
- EU Regulation (EU) 2023/1113 on information accompanying transfers of funds and certain crypto-assets.
- EU GDPR Regulation (EU) 2016/679.
- ESMA and EBA MiCA implementation guidance, applicable national Romanian legislation and regulator publications.

**Open questions:** fixed supply versus minting, secondary-token rights, treasury beneficiary eligibility, user verification, entity domicile, custody model, fundraising and exchange access. These choices materially change legal obligations.
