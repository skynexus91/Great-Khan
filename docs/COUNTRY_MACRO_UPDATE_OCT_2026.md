## IMF October 2026 macro update
The IMF World Economic Outlook October 2026 is available: https://data.imf.org/datasets/WEO . It contains CPI, GDP, debt and other series. Reuters' dated reporting of the IMF October release states:
- **Venezuela:** 2026 average inflation projected at **nearly 468%**. This is an IMF *forecast*, not an observed final 2026 result. 2026 real GDP estimate **not established in this pass**.
- **Argentina:** 2026 inflation forecast **32.5%**; real GDP growth forecast **2.1%**. Both forecasts, not final annual outcomes.
- **Brazil:** 2026 real GDP growth forecast **2.2%**.
- **Latin America and Caribbean aggregate:** 2026 real GDP growth forecast **2.3%**.
Source: Reuters, 2026-10-13: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/imf-cuts-latin-america-caribbean-growth-forecasts-energy-shock-little-ai-boost-2026-10-13/
- **Sub-Saharan Africa aggregate:** 2026 real GDP growth forecast **4.5%**, revised from 4.3%; IMF median inflation expected to rise from 3.4% in 2025 to 5.0% in 2027. These are aggregate/median regional values, not individual-country inflation rates.
Source: Reuters, 2026-10-15: https://www.reuters.com/world/africa/africas-economic-resilience-masks-rising-inflation-threat-fragile-buffers-imf-warns-2026-10-15/
