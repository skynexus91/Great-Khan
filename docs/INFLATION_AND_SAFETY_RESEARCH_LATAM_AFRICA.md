# KHAN — Latin America and Africa: inflation, instability and consumer protection research
Updated 2026-10-19. **This is a humanitarian-access and market-safety research queue, NOT a political-targeting or token-sales list.** No country is approved for launch. Do not target protesters, opposition groups, dissidents or vulnerable people with political messaging or investment pitches.

## Selection principles
Separate **economic need** (inflation, currency instability, remittance cost, payment access) from **political conditions** (documented demonstrations, conflict, restrictions). Do not infer that residents oppose their government. Inflation figures require current national statistical office, IMF or World Bank citations with observation month and measurement method. Protest/security assessments require dated independent reporting; never infer from inflation alone. Political activity is **not** an acquisition signal. High instability can be a reason to defer rollout for user safety.

## Evidence protocol
For every candidate, collect: CPI headline YoY %, CPI month, source URL and publication date; remittance cost and account/mobile-money access; official token/VASP rules and regulator statements; independent dated security/civic reports; sanctions and cross-border constraints; internet/mobile accessibility; humanitarian risk assessment. Tag data VERIFIED / STALE / UNKNOWN and never fabricate a numeric score.

## Ethical and operational safeguards
1. No political persuasion, protest targeting, surveillance, dissident identification, or individualized political profiling.
2. No claims that KHAN is inflation-proof, stable, backed, redeemable or an alternative to local legal tender unless demonstrably true and legally authorized.
3. No exploitation of crisis vulnerability: avoid urgency, speculative returns, and real-money onboarding in fragile environments.
4. Pilot only an educational, non-custodial, no-real-value demo until jurisdiction-specific counsel and independent security review approve a broader scope.
5. Privacy by default: no collection of political affiliation, protest participation, location histories or sensitive identity data for marketing.
6. Humanitarian organizations and local civil society should co-design any pilot; publish transparent opt-in and exit mechanisms.
